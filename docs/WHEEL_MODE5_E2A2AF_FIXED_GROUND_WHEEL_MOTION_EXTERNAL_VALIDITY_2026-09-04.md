## Interpretation

E2a2ae should **not** be dismissed as a `SetTransform(static ground)` artifact. The same recycler-on versus recycler-off divergence survives when the road is truly stationary and the dynamic wheel supplies the relative angular motion, at least for the validated `2 -> 1` crossing.

However, E2a2af does not transfer E2a2ae's *mechanism* automatically. E2a2ae showed that freezing recycled separation reprojection removes the amplification in the transformed-ground apparatus. The next question is whether that same intervention removes the amplification here.
