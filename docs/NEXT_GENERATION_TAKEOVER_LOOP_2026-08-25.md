## 6. Expanded Owner vision — strategic pressure, not architecture

The next-generation JV may grow beyond one car into a broader machine/physics/world sandbox.

Current strategic intent includes:

- high-quality car feel and appearance as the immediate quality anchor;
- easy creation of many different vehicles/machines as a major long-term direction;
- possible drone/flight gameplay as a later second-machine falsifier;
- very fine Owner control over authored components/geometry, potentially near model-pixel-scale authored resolution;
- large photogrammetry worlds as a second strategic pillar, with roughly `2 x 2 km` as a major earlier scale and `10 x 10 km` family-area ambition as a long-term stress target;
- eventual tools that let other users import/prepare their own scanned terrain;
- mobile primarily as a fun/runtime/showcase/share client, not necessarily a full authoring workstation;
- desktop-only advanced authoring is acceptable where small-screen UX would be artificial;
- freedom to redesign or replace current JV/JV-Web foundations when evidence and Owner judgement justify it.

Do **not** respond to this breadth by designing a universal platform upfront. The current car remains the first quality anchor. Promote generalization only after it survives real additional cases.
